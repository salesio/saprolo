Who we are

Your procurement & logistics partner

SAPROLO is a procurement and logistics company operating between South Africa and Mozambique. We help businesses, projects and individuals source products, move them safely across the border and deliver them where they're needed.

Our work is built around the Maputo Development Corridor, one of the busiest trade routes in Southern Africa, linking Gauteng to the Port of Maputo.
